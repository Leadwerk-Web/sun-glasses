# Project Brief — VANTA Eyewear Concept

## Identity and goal

- Project name and visible brand: VANTA Eyewear
- Status: fictional design concept and public static preview; not a registered company or live retail offer
- Value proposition: expressive, lightweight sunglasses edited down to three distinctive silhouettes
- Primary goal: showcase the collection and generate product/fit enquiries after WordPress activation
- Audience: style-conscious adults looking for modern unisex sunglasses
- Source language: English
- Tone: confident, concise, editorial; avoid unsupported sustainability, medical, or performance claims

## Pages and content

- Pages: Home, Frame guide, Our story, Contact, Thank you, Legal notice, Privacy, 404
- Primary CTA: browse the edit, then contact VANTA for fit advice
- Product names, prices, and collection copy are demonstrative concept content
- Photography: locally stored WebP files sourced from Unsplash; creator links appear on the home page

## Brand and media

- Logo and favicon: `assets/logo.svg`, `assets/favicon.svg`
- Palette: black, paper white, electric orange, cobalt blue, acid yellow
- Typeface: system-only Arial/Helvetica stack; no remote fonts
- Default OG image: `assets/og-default.webp`
- Product/editorial images: `assets/cover-solstice-frame.webp`, `assets/editorial-studio-look.webp`, `assets/editorial-black-frame.webp`

## SEO and measurement

- Intent: modern statement sunglasses and frame-shape guidance
- Every HTML document includes a unique Leadwerk focus keyphrase
- No analytics, remote embeds, cookies, or production canonical host in the static source

## Contact, form, and legal status

- Contact form fields: Name, Email, Message, required privacy consent
- Static preview behavior: visual only; no entries are transmitted or stored
- WordPress provider: WPForms Lite 1.10.2+ using source form ID `kontakt-form`
- Thank-you source: `danke.html`; privacy source: `datenschutz.html`
- Production form recipient, legal entity, address, direct contact details, retention terms, and legal review owner must be supplied by the project owner before commercial or WordPress activation

## Special functionality and acceptance

- Progressive enhancement: mobile navigation and reveal animation
- No login, cart, checkout, payments, search, API, or external embed
- No-JS content remains complete; keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior are supported
- GitHub Pages is a non-commercial preview surface only
